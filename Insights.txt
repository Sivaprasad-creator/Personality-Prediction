Stage Fear

Extroverts: 1,312 have No stage fear, while only 105 have stage fear.
Introverts: 986 have stage fear, while only 109 have No stage fear.
This shows a strong relationship between personality and stage fear.
Extroverts generally appear more confident in public situations, while introverts are more likely to experience stage fear.

Drained After Socializing
Extroverts: 1,312 are not drained after socializing, while 105 are drained.
Introverts: 984 are drained after socializing, while 111 are not.
This indicates that introverts are more likely to feel mentally tired after social interaction.
Extroverts generally seem to handle social interaction more comfortably.

Time Spent Alone
Most people spend 0–3 hours alone (1,336).
Extroverts: 1,259 spend 0–3 hours alone.
Introverts: 488 spend 8–11 hours alone.
Introverts clearly tend to spend more time alone, while extroverts prefer less alone time.
There is a strong association between time spent alone and personality type.

Social Event Attendance
Most people attend social events for 0–3 hours (1,107).
Extroverts: 891 spend 4–7 hours and 404 spend 8–10 hours at social events.
Introverts: 985 spend only 0–3 hours at social events.
Extroverts show a much higher tendency to spend longer periods at social events.
Introverts generally prefer shorter social interactions.

Going Outside
Most people go outside for 0–2 hours (1,065).
Extroverts: 867 spend 3–5 hours outside and 446 spend 6–7 hours.
Introverts: 961 spend only 0–2 hours outside.
Extroverts generally spend more time outside, while introverts tend to spend less time outside.
Going-out duration appears to be a useful indicator of personality.

Friends Circle Size
The largest overall group has a friend circle of 0–5 people (1,181).
Extroverts: 696 have 6–10 friends and 522 have 11–15 friends.
Introverts: 982 have only 0–5 friends.
Extroverts tend to have larger friend circles.
Introverts are more likely to maintain a smaller social circle.

Post Frequency
Most people post 0–3 times (1,270).
Extroverts: 785 post 4–7 times and 353 post 8–10 times.
Introverts: 991 post only 0–3 times.
Extroverts appear to be more active on social media.
Introverts generally have lower posting frequency.

Overall Key Insights
Extroverts: Less stage fear, less drained after socializing, less time alone, more social-event attendance, more time outside, larger friend circles, and higher social-media activity.
Introverts: More stage fear, more likely to feel drained after socializing, more time alone, shorter social-event attendance, less time outside, smaller friend circles, and lower posting frequency.
The data shows clear behavioral differences between extroverts and introverts.
Features such as Time Spent Alone, Social Event Attendance, Going Outside, Friends Circle Size, and Stage Fear appear particularly useful for predicting personality.
Overall, the results suggest that social behavior and lifestyle patterns are strongly associated with personality type.
